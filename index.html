Our Story

Slice of New York in the
Heart of Tucson

Empire Pizza was born in 2009 with one obsession: to bring a true, no-compromise New York style pizza to the Sonoran Desert. Every pie is prepared from scratch — dough made fresh daily, loaded with the finest ingredients, crowned with 100% Grande Mozzarella. This is the real thing.
